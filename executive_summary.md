\# Executive Summary



\## Government Fund Utilization and Performance Analysis



\### Overview



This capstone analysis examines 1,000 government fund records across 10 states, 11 schemes, and 5 departments. The analysis covers fund allocation, release, utilization, physical achievement, beneficiary coverage, and project status.



The total sanctioned amount in the dataset is \*\*400,073.92 lakhs\*\*, of which \*\*297,951.32 lakhs\*\* was released and \*\*201,934.77 lakhs\*\* was utilized. The overall utilization of released funds is \*\*67.77%\*\*. The physical achievement rate is \*\*64.18%\*\*, while beneficiary coverage is \*\*61.41%\*\*.



\### Key Findings



Fund utilization varies across states, departments, schemes, and project statuses. The calculated state-level utilization ranges from approximately \*\*59.54% to 73.32%\*\*, showing variation in the proportion of released funds utilized.



Project status is associated with substantial differences in fund utilization. A one-way ANOVA produced an \*\*F-statistic of 673.7816\*\* with a \*\*p-value below 0.001\*\*, indicating statistically significant differences in mean utilization across at least one project-status group.



Fund utilization also shows a positive relationship with operational outcomes. The Pearson correlation between fund utilization and physical achievement is \*\*0.6284 (p < 0.001)\*\*. The correlation between fund utilization and beneficiary coverage is \*\*0.3865 (p < 0.001)\*\*. These results indicate associations within the dataset and do not establish causation.



\### Actionable Recommendations



1\. \*\*Strengthen monitoring of projects with lower utilization and delayed status.\*\* Regular reviews of released funds, utilization progress, and project status can help identify implementation areas requiring follow-up.



2\. \*\*Track financial and operational indicators together.\*\* Fund utilization should be monitored alongside physical achievement and beneficiary coverage so that financial progress is considered together with project outcomes.



3\. \*\*Use state, department, and scheme-level dashboards for periodic performance reviews.\*\* Interactive dashboards can help management compare utilization patterns, identify variations, and prioritize records requiring further investigation.



\### Conclusion



The analysis provides a consolidated view of government fund utilization and related project outcomes. The findings indicate variation in utilization across administrative and project categories and show positive associations between financial utilization, physical achievement, and beneficiary coverage. The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive method for monitoring these indicators and supporting data-driven performance reviews.
