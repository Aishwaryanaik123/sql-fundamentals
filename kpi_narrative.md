# KPI Narrative

## Retail KPI Performance Summary

The KPI dashboard provides an overview of the retail business performance across revenue, profitability, customer behaviour, marketing activity, and inventory.

Revenue Growth is 12%, which is above the target of 10%. Gross Profit Margin is 32%, exceeding the target of 30%.

Average Order Value is ₹1,850 compared with a target of ₹2,000, showing an opportunity to increase the value of individual customer purchases. Conversion Rate is 5.8%, above the target of 5%, while Customer Retention Rate is 82%, above the target of 80%.

Customer Acquisition Cost is ₹920 against a target limit of ₹1,000. Website Traffic Growth is 18%, exceeding the target of 15%. Cart Abandonment Rate is 56%, which is below the target threshold of 60%.

Customer Satisfaction is 88%, exceeding the target of 85%. Inventory Turnover is 5.4 compared with the target of 6, indicating that inventory movement could be improved.

The dashboard shows positive performance across several revenue, customer, and marketing indicators. The main areas requiring attention are Average Order Value and Inventory Turnover, as both are below their respective targets. Monitoring these KPIs regularly can help identify changes in customer purchasing behaviour and inventory efficiency.
