Completed a customer behavioral segmentation deep-dive using the supplied customer profile dataset. Engineered order frequency, total revenue, average order value, tenure, and relative recency features; applied log transformation and standardization; compared K-Means solutions from 2 to 5 clusters using silhouette score; selected five clusters (score 0.3223). Produced customer-level assignments, segment profiles, channel and age-band summaries, data quality checks, methodology and assumptions, recommendations, and a submission-ready PDF report. Churn was excluded from model fitting and used only for descriptive profiling. The property and product datasets were retained but not joined because no shared relationship key was supplied.
