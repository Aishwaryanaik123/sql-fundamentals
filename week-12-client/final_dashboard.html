Definitions, revisions and data governance

KPI glossary: Sold rate = sold listings ÷ total listings. Churn rate = churned customers ÷ total customers. Product gross margin = (MRP − COGS) ÷ MRP; displayed category and overall averages are unweighted means of product-level margins.

Revision R1 — revenue unit: Retained “source units”; currency was not supplied and must be confirmed by the data owner before financial decisions.
Revision R2 — refresh date: Explicitly states that source refresh date was not supplied; no date has been invented.
Revision R3 — KPI definitions: Glossary added above.
Revision R4 — inventory drill-down: Category stock/sales comparison and five-product risk count included. Product-level IDs and stock gaps are not shown because the original product-level CSV was not available in the retrieved project files; no product rows are fabricated.
Revision R5 — city context: City listing counts are displayed with sold rates; differences are descriptive and not adjusted for property mix or transaction mix.
Revision R6 — usability: Responsive chart layout, consistent number labels, mobile-friendly table and chart sizing included. Browser-level accessibility testing is still recommended.
Revision R7 — peer review: The Week 9 record was explicitly marked illustrative, not a real peer review. Actual reviewer feedback remains pending and must not be represented as completed.

Scope: The three source domains have no confirmed shared key and are intentionally kept separate. This dashboard is descriptive, not causal or predictive.
