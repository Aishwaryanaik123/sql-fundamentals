# Recommendation for Business Stakeholders

I recommend **SweetViz** as the first report to share with business stakeholders. Its organized, interactive HTML layout makes it easier to scan feature summaries, distributions, missing values, and relationships without needing to understand Python. AutoViz is valuable for analysts who want to explore a dataset quickly and inspect a wider range of plots, but its output can feel more technical and less focused for a business audience.

For the student-performance dataset, stakeholders should pay particular attention to placement outcomes, salary-package completeness, and differences between academic or college-related groups. Any conclusions should be validated against the source data and business context before decisions are made.
