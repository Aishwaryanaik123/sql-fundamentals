# Storyboard — Week 11 Day 1

## Audience
Business leaders deciding where to focus the next sales-planning cycle.

## One-sentence message
Sales are growing, but seasonality, regional differences, and product concentration mean the next gains require focused planning rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

## Narrative arc: Context → Tension → Insight → Action
| Beat | Purpose | Takeaway | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Lead with answer | Sales have grown across three years | Annual totals |
| Context | Establish trend | Growth continued, but pace changed | YoY growth |
| Tension | Reveal pattern | Sales are stronger near year-end | Monthly averages |
| Insight | Locate differences | Regional performance is uneven | Regional totals and growth |
| Explanation | Show mix | One product category contributes the largest share | Product totals |
| Resolution | Recommend action | Prepare earlier, tailor regionally, diversify | Three proposed actions |
| Close | Secure decision | Approve three initiatives and a one-quarter review | Action plan |

## Key findings
- 2024 growth vs 2023: 9.1%
- 2025 growth vs 2024: 9.5%
- Cumulative growth 2023–2025: 19.5%
- Largest region: West (27.3% share)
- Largest product: Fashion (35.4% share)
- Highest average month: December

## Call to action
1. Prepare inventory and campaigns before the seasonal peak.
2. Investigate regional conversion, availability, and promotion effectiveness.
3. Test category-growth campaigns and evaluate incremental margin.

## Communication principles
Lead with the answer, one point per slide, use takeaway-led titles, explain why each finding matters, and end with a measurable decision.
