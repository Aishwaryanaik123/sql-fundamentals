# Experiment Process — Week 11 Day 1

## Objective
Turn a dataset into a clear business narrative with a beginning, middle, and actionable ending.

## Dataset
Synthetic sales performance from 2023–2025, covering 4 regions and 3 product categories, with 432 records.

## Process
1. Defined the audience as business leaders making sales-planning decisions.
2. Wrote the central message: overall growth hides differences in seasonality, region, and product mix.
3. Calculated annual sales and year-over-year growth.
4. Compared sales by region and product.
5. Calculated average sales by calendar month.
6. Structured the narrative as Context → Tension → Insight → Action.
7. Created a seven-part offline HTML presentation and supporting SVG charts.
8. Documented recommendations, limitations, and a reusable checklist.

## Findings
- 2024 growth: 9.1%; 2025 growth: 9.5%.
- Largest region: West (27.3% of total).
- Largest product: Fashion (35.4% of total).
- Highest average month: December.

## Limitations
Data are synthetic and do not represent a real company. Findings demonstrate narrative structure and chart selection, not actual commercial performance. Validate recommendations against real margin, inventory, customer, and campaign data before business use.
