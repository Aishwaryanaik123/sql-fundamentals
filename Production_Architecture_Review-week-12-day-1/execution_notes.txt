Week 12 Day 1 — Production Architecture Review

Completed a reference architecture review for a production analytics and reporting platform.
Created:
- Stakeholder-ready PDF and editable DOCX report
- Layered architecture diagram and component register
- Architecture risk register and non-functional requirements register
- Production readiness checklist
- Architecture Decision Record (ADR)
- Process documentation

Review themes: ingestion reliability, raw/curated storage, transformation, data quality, security, analytics serving, observability, backup/recovery, CI/CD, and cost controls.

Scope limitation: reference review only. No live infrastructure was inspected or deployed; no security, load, or restore tests were run. Confirm cloud provider, data classification, data volumes, SLAs, RPO/RTO, ownership, and budget with stakeholders.
