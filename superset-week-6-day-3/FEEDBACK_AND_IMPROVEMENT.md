# Dashboard Feedback and Improvement

## Feedback from Colleague
"The dashboard is easy to understand, but the most important KPI should stand out more clearly when the dashboard is opened."

## Improvement Implemented
The dashboard was improved by placing the Overall Placement Rate as a prominent KPI at the top and adding Average Salary as a second KPI. The charts and college-tier table remain below the KPIs so users can first see the headline results and then explore the details.
