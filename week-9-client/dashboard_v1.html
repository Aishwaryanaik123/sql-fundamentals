Interpretation and data notes

Real estate: 600 listings are represented; 209 are marked sold (34.83%). Median listing price is 172.595 lakh and average days on market is 183.7. City comparisons are descriptive and should be interpreted with listing mix in mind.

Customers: 200 customer records contribute 4,992,621 recorded revenue units across 2,307 orders. Six customers are flagged as churned (3%). The dataset does not specify the currency unit.

Products: 50 products contain 13,857 stock units and 5,276 average monthly sales units. Five products have stock below their average monthly sales. Average gross margin is the unweighted average of product-level (MRP − COGS) / MRP.

Scope: These three CSVs represent separate business domains and have no supplied common key for reliable joining. KPIs are therefore presented in separate sections rather than combining unrelated records. This is a descriptive v1 dashboard, not a causal or predictive model.
