# Week 11 Day 5 — Stakeholder Report: 5 Business Insights

## Objective
Turn sales performance data into five clear, evidence-based business insights with stakeholder-ready recommendations.

## Project contents
- `data/sales_performance_2024_2025.csv` — 288 synthetic records for Jan 2024–Dec 2025.
- `reports/stakeholder_report_5_business_insights.pdf` — formatted stakeholder report with KPI snapshot, five insights, charts, methodology, and next steps.
- `reports/five_business_insights.csv` — concise insight/evidence/action register.
- `presentation/stakeholder_report_presentation.pptx` — 8-slide presentation for a stakeholder review.
- `charts/` — five supporting charts.
- `sql/stakeholder_insight_queries.sql` — SQL examples for reproducing the analysis.
- `docs/experiment_process.md` — process and methodology.
- `docs/data_dictionary.md` — field descriptions.
- `execution_notes.txt` — execution summary and limitations.

## Five insights
1. Sales increased year over year by **23.4%** in the sample.
2. **North** is the largest regional contributor (**29.5%** of sales).
3. **Electronics** is the largest product category (**43.1%** of sales).
4. October–December contribute **30.1%** of total sample sales.
5. **East** has the lowest regional profit margin (**15.7%**).

## How to submit
Submit this complete folder. Use the PDF as the primary report and the PowerPoint for verbal presentation. Keep the CSV, SQL, charts, and process notes as supporting evidence.

## Important data note
All records and figures are synthetic and generated for this learning project. They are not actual company performance. Do not present them as real business results. Replace the sample CSV with approved company data if actual findings are required.
