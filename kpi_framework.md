# Retail KPI Framework

## 1. Revenue

- Formula: Total Revenue = Sum of Sales
- Owner: Sales Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: Increase revenue by 10% year-over-year
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## 2. Gross Profit Margin

- Formula: Gross Profit Margin = (Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold) / Revenue × 100
- Owner: Finance Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: Maintain at least 30%
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## 3. Average Order Value (AOV)

- Formula: AOV = Total Revenue / Number of Orders
- Owner: Sales Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: Increase AOV by 8%
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## 4. Conversion Rate

- Formula: Conversion Rate = Number of Purchases / Number of Visitors × 100
- Owner: Marketing Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: Maintain at least 5%
- Type: Leading Indicator

## 5. Customer Retention Rate

- Formula: Retention Rate = (Customers at End - New Customers) / Customers at Start × 100
- Owner: Customer Success Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: Maintain at least 80%
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## 6. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

- Formula: CAC = Total Marketing and Sales Cost / Number of New Customers
- Owner: Marketing Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: Keep CAC below ₹1,000
- Type: Lagging Indicator

## 7. Website Traffic

- Formula: Total Website Traffic = Number of Website Visitors
- Owner: Digital Marketing Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: Increase traffic by 15%
- Type: Leading Indicator

## 8. Cart Abandonment Rate

- Formula: Cart Abandonment Rate = (Carts Created - Completed Purchases) / Carts Created × 100
- Owner: E-commerce Manager
- Frequency: Weekly
- Target: Keep below 60%
- Type: Leading Indicator

## 9. Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)

- Formula: CSAT = Satisfied Responses / Total Responses × 100
- Owner: Customer Experience Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: Maintain at least 85%
- Type: Leading Indicator

## 10. Inventory Turnover Ratio

- Formula: Inventory Turnover = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory
- Owner: Inventory Manager
- Frequency: Monthly
- Target: Maintain a turnover ratio of at least 6
- Type: Lagging Indicator
