Analysis workflow

  1. Load monthly observations and set a regular monthly date index.
  2. Inspect trend using the original series and 12-month rolling mean.
  3. Apply additive decomposition with a 12-month seasonal period.
  4. Fit ARIMA(1,1,1) on the training data.
  5. Evaluate the final 12 months using MAE and RMSE.
  6. Refit on all observations and forecast the next 12 months.

Interpretation and limitations

  • The upward trend is built into the synthetic demonstration data.
  • The annual seasonal component repeats on a 12-month cycle.
  • ARIMA(1,1,1) is a baseline, not a tuned or proven optimal model.
  • Use residual diagnostics and time-aware validation before relying on forecasts.
