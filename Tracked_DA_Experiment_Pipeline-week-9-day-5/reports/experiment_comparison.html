Tracking workflow

  1. Load and validate the synthetic customer activity dataset.
  2. Split the data with a fixed random seed.
  3. Train two baseline classifiers.
  4. Log parameters, evaluation metrics, tags, models, and artifacts to MLflow.
  5. Compare runs and rank by F1 score.
