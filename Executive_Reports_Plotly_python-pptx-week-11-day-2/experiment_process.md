# Experiment Process — Week 11 Day 2

## Objective
Convert sales analysis into executive-ready reporting using Plotly visualizations and a PowerPoint presentation generated with python-pptx.

## Data
Synthetic monthly sales data covering January 2023 to December 2025, with 4 regions and 3 product categories (432 rows).

## Workflow
1. Prepared a synthetic sales dataset.
2. Aggregated total and annual sales, year-over-year growth, regional contribution, product mix, and monthly seasonality.
3. Built interactive Plotly charts and exported them as self-contained HTML files with Plotly JavaScript embedded.
4. Created an 8-slide executive PowerPoint report with KPI cards, trend comparisons, regional and product analysis, seasonality, recommendations, and a decision slide.
5. Documented the narrative, key findings, and data limitations.

## Key findings
- 2024 YoY growth: 9.9%
- 2025 YoY growth: 10.2%
- Largest region: West (27.7% of total sales)
- Largest category: Fashion (34.3% of total sales)
- Peak average sales month: December

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic. Findings demonstrate reporting techniques, not real business performance. Validate recommendations using actual revenue, gross margin, inventory, and customer data before operational use.
